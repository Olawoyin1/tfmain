  Refund Policy
 
Last Updated: [Month Year]

At Talent Factory, we strive to deliver an exceptional learning and career experience for every student. We understand that circumstances may change. This Refund Policy sets out the terms under which refunds or transfers of fees are available, the process for requesting them, and the timelines for resolution. By enrolling in any Talent Factory program, you agree to the terms of this policy.
1.  Application Fees
The application fee for any Talent Factory cohort program is non-refundable and non-transferable in all circumstances. Application fees cannot be transferred from one cohort to another, from one program to another, or from one applicant to another.
By submitting your application, you acknowledge and agree that the application fee is non-refundable, even if your application is withdrawn, incomplete, or unsuccessful.
2.  Tuition Fees
Tuition fees cover access to all learning resources including: live online classes via Zoom, session recordings, the Google Drive resource library, instructor support, the cohort WhatsApp community, and internship placement facilitation.
2.1  Full Refund Window: A student is eligible for a full refund of their initial tuition payment only if a written request is submitted within 48 hours of the payment date. This 48-hour provision applies to the first payment only. All subsequent instalment payments are non-refundable.
2.2  Transfer to Another Cohort: A student may request to transfer their full tuition payment to a future cohort only if they have attended two (2) or fewer live sessions in the current cohort. Beyond the first two sessions, only the pro-rated portion of unused tuition may be transferred to a later cohort.
2.3  Partial Refund on Withdrawal: If a student withdraws after the 48-hour refund window, the unused pro-rated portion of their paid tuition may be eligible for a partial refund at Talent Factory's discretion, subject to the process set out in Clause 6.
2.4  All pro-rated calculations are based on the number of weeks remaining in the learning phase at the time of the withdrawal request, divided by 12 (total learning weeks).
3.  Program Deferment
A student who wishes to defer their enrollment to a later cohort must submit a written deferment request and receive written approval from Talent Factory.
3.1  A student may defer their enrollment only once, and only to the immediately following cohort. Deferment to a cohort more than one cycle ahead is not permitted.
3.2  If the tuition fee for the new cohort is higher than the fee originally paid, the student will be required to pay the difference before the new cohort begins.
3.3  A student who defers must restart the program from Week 1 of the new cohort. They may not continue from a later module based on progress made in their original cohort.
3.4  If the deferred cohort does not proceed for any reason, Talent Factory will work with the student to identify a suitable alternative.
4.  Program Discontinuation
A student who permanently discontinues their enrollment at Talent Factory is not entitled to a refund of the application fee. The unused, pro-rated portion of any paid tuition may be eligible for a partial refund upon written request, subject to Talent Factory's approval as set out in Clause 6.
Students who discontinue any progress, certifications, or internship placement arrangements that were in process at the time of discontinuation.
5.  Scholarships and Sponsored Enrollments
Scholarships or sponsored places awarded by Talent Factory or its partners are subject to specific terms communicated at the time of award. Scholarship recipients are subject to the same tuition refund provisions in Clause 2.
5.1  Scholarships are non-transferable and apply only to the cohort for which they were awarded.
5.2  If a scholarship recipient withdraws, defers, or is dismissed from the program, the scholarship is automatically forfeited. Talent Factory may transfer the scholarship value to another eligible student.
5.3  Sponsored students (those whose fees are paid by a third-party sponsor) must comply with any additional terms set by their sponsor. Refunds to sponsored students will be directed to the sponsoring organisation.
6.  Refund Request Process
To request a refund, transfer, or deferment, students must follow the steps below:
6.1  Submit a Written Request: Email hello@talentfactory.ng with the subject line 'Refund Request — [Your Full Name] — Cohort [Number]'. Include: full name, cohort, reason for request, and any supporting documentation.
6.2  Review & Decision: The Talent Factory operations team will review the request and determine eligibility based on this policy. Talent Factory reserves the right to approve or decline requests at its discretion. Students will be notified of the decision within 14 calendar days of the request.
6.3  Refund Processing: Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method (Paystack) within 10 business days of approval confirmation. Processing timelines may vary depending on your bank or card provider.
6.4  Transfers will apply to the unused portion of the current cohort only. Any fee difference for the new cohort must be settled before the new cohort begins.
7.  Payment Errors
If you believe you have been incorrectly charged, contact hello@talentfactory.ng within 24 hours of the transaction. Include the transaction reference and a description of the error. Talent Factory will investigate and, where the error is confirmed, process the correction using the same payment method.
8.  Limitation of Liability
Talent Factory's total liability to any student for any claim under this Refund Policy shall not exceed the total tuition fees paid by that student for the applicable cohort.
9.  Policy Updates
Talent Factory reserves the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon communication via our website or email. Continued enrollment following such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

This policy governs all formal assessments at Talent Factory, including weekly assignments, the mid-cohort assessment, the Capstone Project, and internship evaluations. It applies to all enrolled students across all cohorts. By enrolling, you agree to the terms set out in this policy.
Talent Factory is committed to assessments that are fair, rigorous, and directly relevant to the HR profession. We do not assess memory recall — we assess applied thinking.
1.  Assessment Structure
The Talent Factory program includes the following formal assessments:

2.  Weekly Assignments
2.1  A practical assignment is set after each weekly module and is due within 5 calendar days of the session.
2.2  Late submissions (received within 48 hours after the deadline) will be accepted with a 20% mark deduction.
2.3  Submissions received more than 48 hours after the deadline will receive a mark of zero but may be completed for learning purposes.
2.4  Students must complete a minimum of 70% of all weekly assignments to be eligible for the Learning Certificate and internship placement.
2.5  Extensions may be granted at the discretion of the Program Coordinator upon written request before the deadline.
3.  Mid-Cohort Assessment — Week 6
3.1  A written case study assignment issued at the end of Week 6. Students have 7 days to complete and submit.
3.2  The case study will require students to analyse a realistic Nigerian HR scenario and present structured recommendations.
3.3  The pass mark for the mid-cohort assessment is 60%.
3.4  Students who score below 60% will receive detailed feedback and may resubmit once within 7 days of receiving their result.
4.  Capstone Project
4.1  The Capstone Project runs across Weeks 9–12 and constitutes 30% of the overall program grade.
4.2  Students must submit a project brief for approval by the end of Week 9. Unapproved projects may not be presented.
4.3  The full project package (7 deliverables) must be submitted no later than 24 hours before the Week 12 presentation session.
4.4  Presentations are delivered live to a panel of senior HR practitioners and are 10–15 minutes in duration, followed by a 5–7 minute Q&A.
4.5  The pass mark for the Capstone Project is 60%. Students who receive below 60% may resubmit a revised version within 14 days of the session. Internship placement will be deferred until the resubmission is assessed.
4.6  Grading Tiers: Distinction (85–100%): Certificate awarded with Distinction, featured at graduation; Pass (60–84%): Certificate issued, eligible for internship; Incomplete (below 60%): Resubmission required.
4.7  Capstone projects must be the student's own original work. See Clause 7 (Academic Integrity) for plagiarism policy.
5.  Internship Evaluations
5.1  Student performance during the internship phase is assessed via: a weekly TF check-in form (student self-report); a mid-internship supervisor evaluation (Week 6 of internship); an end-of-internship supervisor evaluation (Week 12 of internship); a final internship report submitted by the student (Week 12).
5.2  A Pass on the internship phase requires: completion of all 12 weeks; submission of the final report; a satisfactory (pass) rating from the host company supervisor.
5.3  Students who fail the internship phase will receive written feedback. Re-placement in a subsequent cohort's internship cycle may be arranged at Talent Factory's discretion.
6.  Attendance Policy
6.1  Students are expected to attend all 12 live learning sessions. A minimum attendance of 80% is required for the Learning Certificate.
6.2  All sessions are recorded and uploaded to the cohort Drive folder within 24 hours. Missing a session is not a failure — watching the recording counts toward engagement.
6.3  Students who are unable to attend a session must notify the Student Success Coordinator in advance via WhatsApp.
7.  Academic Integrity
Talent Factory takes academic integrity seriously. The following constitute academic misconduct:
7.1  Plagiarism: submitting work that is not your own or that is copied from another source without attribution.
7.2  Collusion: working with another student to produce work that is submitted as your own individual work.
7.3  Fabrication: inventing or misrepresenting data, case studies, or evidence in submitted work.
7.4  Misrepresentation: using AI-generated content as if it were entirely your own work without disclosure or significant adaptation.
Where academic misconduct is suspected: the student will be notified in writing; an investigation will be conducted; a decision and outcome communicated within 7 business days. Outcomes may include: a mark of zero for the affected assessment; requirement to resubmit; suspension from the program; dismissal without refund.
Students found to have committed academic misconduct will have any issued certificates revoked if the misconduct is discovered post-graduation.
8.  Reasonable Adjustments
Students with documented disabilities, health conditions, or exceptional personal circumstances may apply for reasonable adjustments to assessment conditions (e.g. deadline extensions, alternative submission formats). Requests must be made in writing to hello@talentfactory.ng as early as possible and ideally before the relevant assessment begins.
9.  Grade Appeals
9.1  Students who wish to appeal a grade must submit a written appeal to hello@talentfactory.ng within 7 calendar days of receiving their result.
9.2  Appeals must clearly state the grounds for appeal (e.g. administrative error, unreasonable marking, extenuating circumstances not previously considered).
9.3  Appeals will be reviewed by the Operations Manager and a senior instructor not involved in the original marking. A decision will be communicated within 14 calendar days.
9.4  The decision of the appeal panel is final.
10.  Policy Updates
Talent Factory reserves the right to update this Assessment Policy at any time. Students in active cohorts will be notified of any changes that affect their current program cycle. Changes take effect immediately for new cohorts.
