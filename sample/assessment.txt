SECTION A — HR Knowledge
10 questions · 2 points each · 20 points total
Choose the single best answer for each question.

Q1.  What does the abbreviation 'HR' stand for?
A)  Human Rights
B)  Human Resources
C)  Human Relations
D)  Humanistic Research
Answer: A — Human Resources — the function responsible for managing people within an organisation.


Q2.  Which of the following is NOT a core function of the HR department?
A)  Recruitment and selection
B)  Managing accounts payable
C)  Performance management
D)  Learning and development
Answer: B — Accounts payable is a Finance function, not HR.


Q3.  A job description (JD) is BEST described as:
A)  A list of candidates who applied for a role
B)  A document outlining the duties, responsibilities, and requirements of a specific role
C)  A contract signed between an employer and employee
D)  A record of an employee's performance history
Answer: B — A JD defines the role — its responsibilities, reporting lines, and the skills required.


Q4.  What does 'onboarding' mean in an HR context?
A)  The process of dismissing an underperforming employee
B)  The process of integrating a new employee into the organisation
C)  A training programme for senior managers
D)  The process of collecting payroll information
Answer: B — Onboarding is the structured process of helping a new hire settle in, understand the company, and become productive.


Q5.  Which Nigerian government body is responsible for overseeing employee pensions?
A)  NSITF
B)  PenCom
C)  NHF
D)  ITF
Answer: B — PenCom (National Pension Commission) regulates the administration of pensions in Nigeria.


Q6.  The Labour Act in Nigeria primarily governs:
A)  Corporate taxation and business registration
B)  The terms and conditions of employment and employee rights
C)  The registration of trade unions only
D)  Import and export of goods and services
Answer: B — The Labour Act governs employment contracts, wages, working hours, and employee rights.


Q7.  An employee's 'Key Performance Indicators' (KPIs) are best described as:
A)  Personal details recorded in their HR file
B)  Measurable targets linked to their role's goals
C)  The benefits they receive on top of their salary
D)  Their attendance record for the year
Answer: B — KPIs are specific, measurable metrics used to evaluate how well an employee is performing their role.


Q8.  What is a 'probation period' in employment?
A)  A period during which a new employee can earn double pay
B)  A defined initial period during which both employer and employee assess the fit before confirming permanent employment
C)  A disciplinary process for underperforming staff
D)  The notice period required before an employee resigns
Answer: B — Probation is the early assessment phase — typically 3–6 months — before an employee is confirmed as permanent.


Q9.  Which of the following best describes 'employee turnover'?
A)  The number of promotions given in a year
B)  The rate at which employees leave an organisation and are replaced
C)  The process of rotating staff between departments
D)  The total number of employees in a company
Answer: B — Turnover measures how frequently employees are leaving — a high rate is usually a warning sign.


Q10.  What does 'EVP' stand for in an HR context?
A)  Employee Value Proposition
B)  External Vacancy Pool
C)  Executive Verification Process
D)  Employee Verification Protocol
Answer: A — EVP — Employee Value Proposition — is the sum of benefits, culture, and rewards that an employer offers in exchange for the employee's skills and contribution.



SECTION B — Situational Judgement
10 questions · 2 points each · 20 points total
Each question presents a realistic HR scenario. Choose the response that represents the most professionally appropriate action.

Q11.  A manager tells you an employee has been 'lazy' and wants to fire them immediately. As an HR professional, what should you do FIRST?
A)  Agree with the manager and send a termination letter
B)  Ask the manager to document specific performance concerns and check if a Performance Improvement Plan has been attempted
C)  Tell the employee they are being fired
D)  Do nothing — it is the manager's decision
Answer: B — A fair process requires documentation and typically a PIP before termination. Acting on a manager's opinion without evidence exposes the company to legal risk.


Q12.  An employee approaches you in confidence to say they are being harassed by a colleague. You are required to:
A)  Keep it completely confidential and take no action unless the employee files a formal complaint
B)  Document the conversation and follow the company's grievance policy, explaining to the employee what will happen next
C)  Tell the employee to resolve it directly with the colleague
D)  Report it immediately to the CEO before speaking further with the employee
Answer: B — HR must follow the grievance process. Confidentiality has limits — you must take action while keeping the employee informed.


Q13.  A department head wants to post a job advert specifying 'female applicants only'. What do you advise?
A)  Allow it — the department head knows best
B)  Refuse and explain that it is discriminatory under Nigerian Labour Law unless there is a legally valid exception
C)  Allow it as long as it is not published externally
D)  Approve it and review the applications before making any changes
Answer: B — Discriminatory job adverts violate equal opportunity principles and Nigerian Labour Law. HR must advise against it.


Q14.  You notice that the same employee has been absent every Monday for the past 6 weeks. What is the appropriate HR response?
A)  Immediately issue a query letter
B)  Have an informal, empathetic conversation with the employee first to understand the pattern, then follow up formally if needed
C)  Deduct pay immediately without discussion
D)  Send an email to their manager without speaking to the employee
Answer: B — A pattern absence warrants a private conversation before formal action — there may be a health, personal, or wellbeing issue involved.


Q15.  A new employee completes their 3-month probation and their manager wants to extend it without giving any reasons. What should you advise?
A)  Approve the extension — probation can be extended at any time for any reason
B)  Advise that an extension requires documented, specific performance concerns communicated to the employee, with clear targets and a timeline
C)  Tell the employee their probation is extended and move on
D)  Confirm the employee's employment without telling the manager
Answer: B — Extending probation without documented reasons and clear communication to the employee is unfair and potentially unlawful.


Q16.  An employee is made redundant from their role. Under Nigerian law, what are they typically entitled to?
A)  Nothing — redundancy is entirely at the employer's discretion
B)  A minimum of one month's notice or pay in lieu, and severance pay based on years of service
C)  Only their remaining salary — no additional entitlements
D)  Three months' notice regardless of how long they have worked
Answer: B — The Labour Act specifies notice entitlements and severance obligations for redundancy situations.


Q17.  A hiring manager wants to hire a candidate they met at a networking event without posting the job publicly. Your recommended approach is:
A)  Approve it — if the manager is confident, the process is not necessary
B)  Advise that a competitive process should still be followed or at minimum documented to protect against claims of unfair hiring
C)  Refuse to process the hire entirely
D)  Tell the manager to post the job, review applications, but then still hire their contact
Answer: B — Good practice requires documented, fair selection — even informal hires should have a minimum process on record.


Q18.  An employee resigns and threatens to share negative information about the company on social media. What is the correct HR response?
A)  Threaten legal action immediately
B)  Conduct a calm, professional exit conversation. Address legitimate grievances. Do not make threats. Ensure final pay is processed correctly.
C)  Immediately withhold their final paycheck
D)  Ignore the threat completely
Answer: B — Professional handling of exits — even difficult ones — protects the company's reputation. Legal threats often escalate the situation.


Q19.  You are reviewing CVs for a role and notice an applicant's name suggests a particular ethnicity. You should:
A)  Remove the application from consideration
B)  Consider the CV purely on qualifications and experience — ethnicity is irrelevant to role fitness
C)  Ask the hiring manager if they have a preference
D)  Flag the application as high-risk
Answer: B — Recruitment decisions must be based on skills and fit — not ethnicity, religion, gender, or any protected characteristic.


Q20.  An employee asks for a copy of their personal data held by the company. Under the NDPR, you should:
A)  Refuse — HR files are confidential
B)  Comply with the request within a reasonable timeframe (typically 30 days) by providing access to the data the company holds on them
C)  Ask the CEO before responding
D)  Only provide payroll data, nothing else
Answer: B — Nigeria's Data Protection Regulation gives individuals the right to access their personal data. HR must comply.



SECTION C — Written Thinking
5 questions · 2 points each · 10 points total
These questions have no single correct answer. Write honest, thoughtful responses in the space provided. Responses are assessed on clarity, relevance, and depth of thinking.

Q21.  In your own words, describe one challenge facing HR professionals in Nigerian organisations today. (Max 3 sentences)
A)  Sample strong answer: Nigerian HR professionals often struggle with enforcing formal HR processes in organisations where personal relationships and hierarchy override structure. Compliance with labour law is inconsistently applied, particularly in SMEs. There is also a shortage of HR-trained professionals relative to the growing number of companies.
B)  Weak answer: HR in Nigeria is difficult because of many problems.
C)  Very weak: Nigeria has a lot of challenges.
D)  No attempt.
Answer: A — Strong answers demonstrate awareness of the Nigerian HR context — structural, cultural, or compliance challenges. Award 2 pts for specific + contextual response, 1 pt for vague but relevant.


Q22.  What do you think is the most important quality an HR professional must have, and why? (Max 2 sentences)
A)  Sample strong answer: The ability to hold trust — from employees and leadership simultaneously — because HR's value depends on people believing they are fair, discreet, and acting in the company's genuine interest.
B)  Weak: You need to be nice and helpful.
C)  Very weak: Communication.
D)  No attempt.
Answer: A — Strong answers go beyond generic traits (communication, organisation) and show understanding of HR's unique dual role. Award 2 pts for specific insight, 1 pt for basic but relevant.


Q23.  A company you have just joined has no HR policies at all. What would be the FIRST three policies you would write and why?
A)  Sample strong: 1) Leave policy — to avoid disputes about absence; 2) Disciplinary procedure — to ensure fair handling of conduct issues; 3) Anti-harassment policy — to create a safe workplace and reduce legal risk.
B)  Weak: I would write a recruitment policy, training policy, and pay policy.
C)  Very weak: I don't know.
D)  No attempt.
Answer: A — Strong answers show priority reasoning — why those three specifically? Award 2 pts for three policies with clear rationale tied to risk or employee experience. 1 pt for three policies without reasoning.


Q24.  If an employee told you they were being underpaid compared to a colleague in the same role, how would you handle the situation?
A)  Sample strong: I would first treat the conversation with confidentiality. I would review both employees' pay data against the job grade and conduct a market benchmark. If a genuine pay gap exists, I would escalate to the relevant manager with a recommendation. I would follow up with the employee to close the loop regardless of outcome.
B)  Weak: I would tell the manager and let them decide.
C)  Very weak: Tell them salaries are private.
D)  No attempt.
Answer: A — Strong answers demonstrate a structured, fair, and confidential approach. Award 2 pts for process-driven response. 1 pt for partial/incomplete process.


Q25.  Why do you want to build a career in Human Resources? (Max 3 sentences — be specific)
A)  Award full marks for answers that are personal, specific, and connect HR to a genuine career goal or experience.
B)  Award partial marks for general interest without specificity.
C)  Award zero for non-answers or purely generic statements.
D)  No attempt.
Answer: A — This is assessed on authenticity and specificity. Generic motivations ('I like people') score lower than specific ones ('I want to solve the retention problem I watched my manager struggle with').
